## License Holders

The license identifies Hiddenmy and TariqIsHuman as the primary licensor parties.

## What Counts as a Finished Project

A finished project is a deployed website or app made for one brand, company, or client.

Examples:

- A hosting provider website.
- A client storefront.
- A custom production deployment.
- A private internal demo for one licensed project.

A finished project is not a reusable theme, starter kit, template marketplace item, UI kit, or source-code package.

## You May

- Use Sonata for your own websites.
- Use Sonata for client websites.
- Modify Sonata for licensed projects.
- Deploy finished websites and applications.
- Make private backups for internal development.

## You May Not

- Resell Sonata.
- Redistribute Sonata.
- Sublicense Sonata.
- Leak or share the source.
- Publish a nulled or cracked release.
- Sell a modified copy as another theme.
- Package it as a starter kit, UI kit, template, or source-code product.
- Remove or tamper with license markers, download identifiers, or anti-piracy placeholders.

## Client Work

You may use Sonata to build a finished project for a client.

You may not give the client reusable Sonata source files in a way that allows them to reuse, resell, redistribute, or create separate products from Sonata unless they have their own valid license or written permission.

## Backups and Private Repositories

You may keep private backups and private repositories for licensed work.

Make sure:

- Repository access is limited to people working on the licensed project.
- Public forks are disabled.
- Build artifacts do not expose private source.
- License markers remain intact.

## Extended Rights

If you need resale, redistribution, marketplace bundling, or white-label rights, request written permission from Hiddenmy and TariqIsHuman.

## Third-Party Packages

Sonata depends on third-party packages. Those packages remain under their own licenses.

The Sonata Commercial License applies to Sonata itself and derivative Sonata materials, not to third-party packages beyond what their own licenses allow.

## When to Ask for Permission

Ask before:

- Bundling Sonata into another product.
- Selling a modified copy.
- Giving reusable source access to another party.
- Publishing the source in a public repository.
- Removing marketplace or license markers.
