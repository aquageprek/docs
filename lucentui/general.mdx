Open **Extensions → LucentUI Editor**. Global behavior is split between the **Colors**
and **Typography & Layout** tabs.

## Theme behavior

| Control | Options | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Default Theme | Auto, Light, Dark | Initial choice for first-time visitors |
| Force Theme | User choice, Force Light, Force Dark | Overrides visitor preference and hides the toggle when forced |
| Design Mode | Lucent UI, Flat UI | Changes surface rendering without changing content |
| Disable Homepage | On/off | Sends visitors to the dashboard instead of the marketing homepage |

Returning visitors keep their own light/dark/auto selection unless **Force Theme** is
active.

## Typography

LucentUI separates body and heading typography:

- **Body Font** applies to paragraphs and interface chrome.
- **Heading Font** applies to headings and branded navigation text. Leave it empty to
  reuse the body font.
- **Heading Weight** supports 600, 700, 800, and 900.
- **Base Font Size** provides compact (14 px), default (16 px), and comfortable (18 px)
  scaling.

Available font families include Figtree, Plus Jakarta Sans, Poppins, Inter, Roboto,
Montserrat, Lato, Open Sans, IBM Plex Sans Thai, and Orbitron. The browser uses the
configured fallback stack when a remote font cannot load.

## Navigation shell

The navbar and authenticated sidebar share the same content but offer different shells:

- Navbar shape: Floating pill, Flat bar, Solid bar, or Minimal rule.
- Navbar alignment: Left Aligned or Centered.
- Sidebar shape: Floating or Docked.
- Authenticated users can collapse the sidebar into a 72 px icon rail; the choice persists
  across reloads.

See [Navigation and footer](/lucentui/navigation) for custom links and visibility controls.

## Display controls

- Show full logo or icon-only branding.
- Show the account credit balance.
- Add the active currency code to the language indicator.
- Show or hide cart, notifications, and guest login/register actions.
- Enable the cookie notice and position it bottom-left or bottom-right.

The empty cart slot collapses automatically; disabling the cart is only necessary when
the store should never expose it.

## Cookie notice

Configure the title, body, button text, and side in **Typography & Layout → Cookie Banner
Text**. Acceptance is saved in the visitor's browser.

<Tip>
  Describe the cookies your Paymenter installation actually uses and link to a privacy
  policy in the footer. The built-in notice is a presentation tool, not a substitute for
  legal review or a full consent-management platform.
</Tip>
