LucentUI renders one component tree through two visual systems. Switching appearance
does not replace homepage blocks, dashboard blocks, copy, media, colors, or extension
data.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Lucent UI">
    The default design uses restrained translucent surfaces, semantic borders, and
    background blur. Choose a surface hierarchy and glass recipe to control how often
    and how strongly glass appears.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Flat UI">
    Flat mode removes backdrop blur and ambient glass depth across the theme. Panels stay
    opaque and continue using the configured light and dark palette.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Lucent surface hierarchy

| Option | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| Balanced | Quiet content cards with glass reserved for navigation and emphasis |
| Full Glass | A consistent frosted treatment across most surfaces |
| Solid | Opaque surfaces while retaining the Lucent layout language |
| Minimal | Transparent cards with restrained separators |

Balanced is the safest default for readability and visual hierarchy.

## Glass recipes

| Preset | Blur | Light surface | Dark surface |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Subtle & Crisp | 12 px | 15% | 12% |
| Balanced Lucent | 16 px | 20% | 20% |
| Deep Frost | 24 px | 30% | 32% |

Optional custom blur is clamped to 0–50 px. Custom opacity is clamped to 10–100%.
Leave either field empty to inherit the chosen preset.

## Flat surface styles

| Option | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Bordered | Crisp opaque cards with clear outlines |
| Filled | Tonal opaque panels with softer separation |
| Quiet | Minimal chrome and fewer visible containers |

These controls appear only while Flat UI is selected.

## Choosing a mode

- Use **Balanced Lucent** when wallpaper and branded depth are part of the experience.
- Use **Flat / Bordered** for dense business dashboards and maximum separation.
- Use **Flat / Quiet** when content hierarchy already provides enough structure.
- Validate light and dark mode after every palette or wallpaper change.

<Info>
  “Solid” under Lucent surface hierarchy and “Flat UI” are not identical. Solid retains
  the Lucent system and layout treatment; Flat UI disables the glass behavior globally
  and offers its own three surface styles.
</Info>
